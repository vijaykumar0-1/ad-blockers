! Title: LocalBlock fixes
! LocalBlock's own filters, for sites the community lists don't cover yet.
! Same syntax as uBlock Origin's lists. Compiled by tools/build-rules.mjs with the others.
!
! hianime: "ad gate" buttons and clicks anywhere open a new tab through a same-site
! bridge (/continue) that the server redirects to rotating ads. The script clicks a hidden
! <a target="_blank"> during the user's click, with window.open as a fallback.
! 1) don't let the page click those links or window.open them
hianime.*##+js(lb-no-anchor-click-if, /\/continue(?:[?#]|$)/)
hianime.*##+js(nowoif, /\/continue(?:[?#]|$)/)
! 2) if a tab opens anyway, close it
/continue?$popup,domain=hianime.*
/continue|$popup,domain=hianime.*
! 3) and never let the bridge load (Safari reports a new tab's address late)
||hianime.ms/continue^$document
||hianime.to/continue^$document
||hianime.nz/continue^$document
||hianime.se/continue^$document
||hianime.sx/continue^$document
||hianime.bz/continue^$document
